How to play

  • Click a difference on either picture. Found ones get a gold circle on both panels and a green check.
  • Find them all before the timer empties. A wrong guess costs −5s and breaks your streak.
  • Streaks pay: each find is +100, plus +25 per streak step. Clearing a scene banks +5 per second left.
  • Stuck? 💡 Hint (H) pulses one unfound difference — 3 per scene. 🔍 Lens (L) magnifies under your cursor.
  • Keyboard: arrows move the crosshair · Enter guesses · Tab switches picture · H hint · L lens · N next scene · R restart · 1–6 pick a scene · P pause.
  • Differences are fair: something removed, recoloured, moved, resized, flipped, or added. 48 in total across 6 scenes, harder as you go.
